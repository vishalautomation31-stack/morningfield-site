Privacy

Last updated 17 October 2026.

This page covers Morningfield Ventures Private Limited, registered at the address on the home page, and the two brands it runs, The Morning Beer and MorningLabs AI.

This website

This website has no forms, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising cookies. Our hosting provider may set technical cookies that are needed to deliver and protect the site. We do not collect your name or email address unless you write to us.

When you email us

If you email finance@morningfieldventures.com, we keep your message and our reply so that we can answer you and keep a record. We do not add you to a mailing list unless you ask.

Customer data in the tools we build for clients

For some clients we build messaging and sales tools, such as a team inbox that receives WhatsApp messages. In those tools we handle the personal data of the client's customers: names, phone numbers, the messages they send, and the notes the client's staff add. We do this on the client's behalf and on the client's instructions, to run the tool the client asked for. The client decides why that data is collected. We do not use it for our own marketing and we do not sell it.

Messages that travel over WhatsApp also pass through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform, which has its own terms and privacy notice.

Who can see the data

Access is limited to the client's staff and to the people at Morningfield Ventures who build and support that tool. The data is held with cloud providers chosen for each project.

Asking us to delete or correct your data

If your phone number or messages are in a tool we built for a client and you want them deleted or corrected, email finance@morningfieldventures.com from any address and tell us the phone number concerned. We will pass your request to the client and help them carry it out.

How long data is kept

Data is kept for as long as the client uses the tool. It is deleted, or handed back to the client, when the work ends.
